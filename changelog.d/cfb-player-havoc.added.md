- **CFB player havoc:** `cfb.cfb_player_havoc(seasons)` gives each defender's tackles for loss, sacks, pass
  breakups, interceptions and forced fumbles per game, with season `_rank` / `_pct` / `_n` (from 6 games).
  Counting stats from ESPN's box and play text, not a grade, and no PFF data. (#1242)
