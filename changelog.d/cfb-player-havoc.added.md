- **CFB player havoc:** `cfb.cfb_player_havoc(seasons)` gives each defender's tackles for loss, sacks, pass
  breakups, interceptions and forced fumbles per season, each per game with its season `_rank` / `_pct` / `_n`
  (ranked from 6 games). The first four come from ESPN's player box and forced fumbles from the play text. These
  are counting stats, not a grade, and use no PFF data. Team havoc stays a share of plays.
