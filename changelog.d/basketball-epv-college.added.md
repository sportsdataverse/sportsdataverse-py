- **College basketball:** `mbb_epv_plays` / `wbb_epv_plays` give EPV and EPV added on every men's and women's
  college play-by-play event, from boosters fitted on 2022-24 and gated on the 2025 and 2026 seasons (BB-D24). (#1118)
- **College basketball:** `mbb_epv_player_games` / `_lineup_games` / `_team_games` (and the `wbb_epv_*` twins) roll
  EPV added up to each player's own actions and on-court +/-, each unit and each team-game (BB-D24). (#1118)
- **Basketball:** `nba_epv.epv_level` scales a booster's EPV by the season's points per possession before each
  game; the college boosters carry it, the NBA booster does not (BB-D24). (#1118)
