- **International basketball:** 530 wrappers over 24 providers (28 flat-API stems), each family in its own
  `sportsdataverse.basketball.<home>` package with parsers and a throttled runtime: FIBA (`fiba_gdap`,
  `fiba_gamepage`), Genius Sports LiveStats, Sportradar Atrium, NBL Australia, FEB, BBL/DBBL (`scb`), Lega Basket,
  ACB, IBBA, KZS, LKL, ABA League, BNXT (`sportpress`), Basketligaen (`sportality`), VTB, W League, KBL, B.LEAGUE,
  CBA, TBF, CEBL, TPBL, P. LEAGUE+ and EASL. Payloads are fetched from public sources and are not redistributed
  as release assets; each family's docstring notes carry its terms-of-use flag.
