- **Basketball:** `{nba,wnba,mbb,wbb}_team_game_possessions` give the season team rates' possession
  counts per team, game and split (`poss`, `opp_poss`, `pts`, `opp_pts`, `seconds`); a split's rows add up
  to the team's season row.
- **Basketball:** `{nba,wnba,mbb,wbb}_situational_team_games` give the team situational splits one game at
  a time; a split's rows add up to the team's `*_situational_team` season row.
