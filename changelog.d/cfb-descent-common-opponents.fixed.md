- **CFB:** in `cfb_standings()`, the SEC, Big Ten, ACC and Big 12 "record against common opponents in
  order of finish" tiebreaker now compares only opponents that every tied team played, as those
  conferences' published policies require. Before, a tier counted as soon as each tied team had played
  any one team in it. The real 2025 ACC title-game berth now goes to Duke (it went to Georgia Tech),
  and the 2025 SEC pairing is Alabama then Georgia (it was Georgia then Ole Miss), both matching the
  real title games. The Sun Belt's "next highest position" rung is unchanged. (#1140)
