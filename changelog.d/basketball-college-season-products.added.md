- **College basketball:** `mbb_season_projections` / `wbb_season_projections` run a seeded Monte
  Carlo of the rest of a season: projected W-L, conference standings, title, auto-bid, at-large and
  bid %, and the seed distribution (BB-D17) (#1079)
- **College basketball:** `mbb_strength_of_schedule` (and `wbb_`) adds the NCAA RPI, a NET-style
  estimate and NET-ranked quads with `_rank` / `_pct` / `_n`, and takes an `as_of_date` (BB-D17) (#1079)
- **College basketball:** `mbb_bracketology` (and `wbb_`) seeds the First Four, labels the bubble
  and gives auto-bids to the conference-tournament champions (BB-D17) (#1079)
