- **NFL:** the current season flips on the Wednesday after Labor Day and week 1 starts on the Thursday after it (nflreadr's
  rules; week read one high in 2015-16, 2020-22, 2026-27); `nfl_schedule.most_recent_nfl_season` and `nfl_schedule.get_current_week`
  are that one rule, so from September 1 to that Wednesday they give the previous season (week 22), not the new one. (#791)
