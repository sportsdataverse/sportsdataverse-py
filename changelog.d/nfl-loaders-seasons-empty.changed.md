- NFL loaders follow nflreadpy's `seasons` contract: `seasons=None` loads the current season, `seasons=True` loads every season from the loader's first through the current one (skipping only a current season that is not published yet), and `seasons=[]` returns an empty frame without a request. `None` used to raise `TypeError` in every helper-backed loader, and in `load_nfl_player_stats` (where it is the default) it loaded every season since 1999 (~28 assets); `load_nfl_player_stats(False)` still raises `TypeError`. `load_nfl_rosters` and `load_nfl_depth_charts` take the current season on the roster calendar (the year turns on March 15), as nflreadpy's `load_rosters` / `load_depth_charts` do. `[]` used to return every season from `load_nfl_schedule`, `load_nfl_nextgen_stats`, season-level `load_nfl_pfr_advstats` and `load_nfl_espn_qbr`, and to raise from `load_nfl_ratings_weekly` / `load_nfl_ngs`. A mistyped season (`2024.5`, `True` in a list, `"2024x"`) raises `ValueError` naming `seasons`. `load_nfl_nextgen_stats` now raises `SeasonNotFoundError` for a season before 2016, like every other NFL loader.
