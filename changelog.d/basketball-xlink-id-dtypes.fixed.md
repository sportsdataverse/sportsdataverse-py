- `load_nba_team_crosswalk`, `load_nba_schedule_crosswalk` and `load_nba_player_crosswalk` return the NBA Stats bridge ids (`nba_team_id`, `nba_home_team_id` / `nba_away_team_id`, `nba_player_id`) as Int64, the dtype NBA Stats ships `team_id` / `player_id` with, as the WNBA crosswalks already did; the zero-padded `nba_game_id` stays String. The `load_ncaa_wbb_pbp` / `_player_box` / `_team_box` / `_possessions` / `_shots` loaders cast their `espn_*` ids to Int32, as the `load_ncaa_mbb_*` ones do. `ncaa_espn_team_crosswalk` returns `espn_team_id` as Int32, the dtype of the crosswalk and NCAA releases, and `return_as_pandas=True` keeps nullable Int32 ids as pandas `Int32` instead of float.
