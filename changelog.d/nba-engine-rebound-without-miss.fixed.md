- **NBA / WNBA / G League possessions:** a rebound row that follows no miss no longer opens a possession for the
  team that just had the ball: a putback's rebound logged after it stays in the putback's possession, and a
  placeholder or post-steal rebound does not set the offense, so the next basket is not lost. (#1134)
- **NBA / WNBA / G League possessions:** a defense `violation` row (a delay of game) no longer names the offense of
  an end-of-period group; the other team's heave does (4 NBA 2025-26 possessions). (#1134)
