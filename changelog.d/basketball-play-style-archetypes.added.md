- **Basketball:** `nba_player_play_styles` gives each player-season an offensive and a defensive play-style
  archetype (Synergy mix, shot profile, tracking; matchups, hustle), `nba_team_play_styles` a defensive one (BB-D20). (#1112)
- **College basketball:** `mbb_play_styles` / `wbb_play_styles` extend the box archetypes with shot (assisted,
  dunk shares) and lineup-role features; KMeans centers fit on training seasons, frozen and applied as-of. (#1112)
