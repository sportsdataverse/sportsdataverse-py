- **Basketball:** `nba_coach_seasons` / `nba_coach_careers`, `wnba_*`, `mbb_*` and `wbb_*` give head-coach seasons and
  careers joined to team results: W-L, points, ORtg/DRtg/net, pace and the four factors over the games each coach was on
  the bench for, with `_rank`, `_pct` and `_n` (BB-D8). Games are credited per game to the coach of record:
  stats.nba.com's staff names the pro coaches and ESPN's per-coach records split a season that changed coach; college
  coaches come from the stats.ncaa.org coach cards (`ncaa_team_coaches`), not KenPom. (#NNN)
