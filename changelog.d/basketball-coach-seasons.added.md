- **Basketball:** `{nba,wnba,mbb,wbb}_coach_seasons` and `_coach_careers` give head-coach seasons and
  careers joined to team results: W-L, points, ORtg/DRtg/net, pace and the four factors over the games
  each coach was on the bench for, with `_rank`, `_pct` and `_n` (BB-D8). (#1109)
- **Basketball:** coach games are credited per game to the coach of record: stats.nba.com's staff and
  ESPN's per-coach records for the pros, the stats.ncaa.org coach cards (`ncaa_team_coaches`) for
  college, not KenPom. (#1109)
