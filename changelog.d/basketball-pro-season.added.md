- **Basketball:** `nba_season_sim` and `wnba_season_sim` simulate the rest of a regular season as of a date
  (seeded Monte Carlo on the BB-D19 possession ridge): projected W-L, division, seed, top-6, play-in, playoff
  and draft-lottery odds with their Monte Carlo error. `nba_season_standings` / `wnba_season_standings` apply
  the leagues' tiebreakers, and `nba_draft_lottery_odds` / `wnba_draft_lottery_odds` give the exact lottery
  tables (BB-D18).
