- `ncaa_espn_player_crosswalk(season, league)` (`sportsdataverse.mbb` / `sportsdataverse.wbb`): a season crosswalk from the stats.ncaa.org player id to the ESPN athlete id, built from published releases (the NCAA team roster, `ncaa_espn_team_crosswalk`, the ESPN player box score), with `match_method`, `match_confidence` and `unmatched_reason`. It matches 99.7-99.9% of 2023-24 and 2024-25 roster players in both leagues and agrees row for row with the `source == "ncaa"` rows of `load_{mbb,wbb}_player_aliases`.
