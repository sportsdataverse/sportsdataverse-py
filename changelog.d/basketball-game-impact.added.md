- **Basketball:** `{nba,wnba,mbb,wbb}_game_player_impact` give the game impact box (minutes, on-court
  possessions, +/- and ORtg/DRtg/net with `_n` per player and game), and `*_game_lineups_used` the
  five-man units of each game (BB-D6). (#1103)
- **Basketball:** points are credited per scoring action and free throws to the lineup at the foul;
  the +/- equals stats.nba.com's box on 91% of 2025-26 NBA and 98.5% of 2026 WNBA player-games, the
  rest being on-court lineup errors in the source. (#1103)
