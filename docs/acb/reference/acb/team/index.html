ACB — ACB API (api2.acb.com, Spain) — Team

acb_team_box​

Team totals of a game per period: everything the team did, and the part credited to the team itself.

Endpoint URL: GET https://api2.acb.com/api/matchdata/Result/boxscores

Valid URL: https://api2.acb.com/api/matchdata/Result/boxscores?matchId=105401

API ParameterPythonPatternRequiredNullableDescription
matchIdmatch_idYRequired. ACB match id (integer), one global sequence for every competition: legacy ids (seasons up to 2019-20) sit below about 20000 (12559 = the 2005-06 final); ids from 2020-21 start around 100000 (105401 = Barca 89-86 Valencia Basket, Liga 2026-27 round 3).

Returns​

return_parsed=True (default) — a tidy polars.DataFrame with the columns below; pass return_as_pandas=True for a pandas.DataFrame.

col_nametypedescription
match_idInt64ACB match id (one global sequence for every competition; legacy ids are below about 20000 up to 2019-20, ids from 2020-21 start around 100000). Taken from the request when the body does not name the game.
edition_idInt64Season id (editionId) = season START year - 1935 (91 = 2026-27). The wrappers take the END year, season.
seasonInt64Season END year (2027 = 2026-27) = edition_id + 1936: the key the wrappers take.
team_sideStringSide of the game the team played: home or away (the feed lists the home team first).
team_idInt64Season-team entry id: one club in one competition edition, NEW every season (Barca = 4467 in Liga 2026-27). Not the club: use club_id to follow a club across seasons.
club_idInt64Stable ACB club id (2 = Barca, 9 = Real Madrid, 13 = Valencia Basket): the same across seasons and sponsor names, and the club_id request argument.
teamStringTeam name as registered for that season (sponsor names change; the club id does not).
team_abbrStringThree-letter team abbreviation (BAR, RMB, VBC).
quarterInt64Period: 0 = the whole game, 1-4 the quarters, 5+ overtimes (games before about 2010 carry only 0).
scopeStringtotal = everything the team did (its players plus the team's own events); team = the part credited to the team itself rather than a player (team rebounds, team turnovers).
pointsInt64Points, team total (players + team)
free_throws_madeInt64Free throws made, team total (players + team)
free_throws_attemptedInt64Free throws attempted, team total (players + team)
two_pointers_madeInt64Two-point field goals made, team total (players + team)
two_pointers_attemptedInt64Two-point field goals attempted, team total (players + team)
three_pointers_madeInt64Three-point field goals made, team total (players + team)
three_pointers_attemptedInt64Three-point field goals attempted, team total (players + team)
assistsInt64Assists, team total (players + team)
off_reboundsInt64Offensive rebounds, team total (players + team)
def_reboundsInt64Defensive rebounds, team total (players + team)
total_reboundsInt64Total rebounds, team total (players + team)
stealsInt64Steals, team total (players + team)
turnoversInt64Turnovers, team total (players + team)
blocksInt64Shots blocked (by this player or team), team total (players + team)
received_blocksInt64Own shots blocked by an opponent, team total (players + team)
dunksInt64Dunks, team total (players + team)
personal_foulsInt64Fouls committed, team total (players + team)
fouls_drawnInt64Fouls received, team total (players + team)
plus_minusInt64Point differential while on court (+/-), team total (players + team)
ratingInt64Valoracion: ACB's efficiency rating (PIR) = points + rebounds + assists + steals + blocks + fouls received - missed field goals - missed free throws - turnovers - shots blocked by an opponent - fouls committed, team total (players + team)

return_parsed=False — the raw body (dict): the route's JSON plus _request (the ids asked for, in snake_case, with season the END year).

Raises​

ExceptionDescription
NoDataErrorThe host answered 404 -- the requested resource does not exist. The ACB has nothing for the request (an unknown id, play-by-play or shots before 2016-17, a club with no games that season); a game outside a playoff series is zero rows.
AssetFetchErrorThe fetch failed (a non-2xx answer or a connection failure after retries, e.g. 401/403/429/5xx, or an empty or unreadable 200 body) -- the answer is unknown, not empty. The fetch failed or the answer is not the one asked for (a rejected key, an error envelope or stub, a body for another season, round, club or player, a game that is not finished).
ValueErrorThe host answered 400 / 422 -- the request is wrong; retrying cannot help. A missing or non-integer id, or a season before 1984 (raised before any request), or the API's HTTP 400 / 422.

acb_team_box(match_id=105401)
