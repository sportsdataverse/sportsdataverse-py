nba_coach_seasons​

nba_coach_seasons(seasons: 'int | list[int]', *, league_id: 'str' = '00', season_type: 'str' = 'regular-season', espn: 'bool' = True, return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

Head-coach seasons: W-L, ratings and four factors over the games each coach was on the bench for.

One row per season, team and head coach. A coach who took over mid-season gets his games only. Who coached each game: see sportsdataverse.nba.nba_coach_seasons.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
seasonsint | list[int]A season or list of seasons, named by the year it ends (2026 is 2025-26). The WNBA season is its calendar year.
league_idstr'00'"00" NBA or "10" WNBA.
season_typestr'regular-season'"regular-season" (default), "playoffs" or "play-in"; the postseason games go to the coach of the team's last regular-season game.
espnboolTrueRead ESPN's per-coach records (2002 on) to split a team-season that changed coach (about 90 requests a season). False credits only the seasons one coach had throughout.
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas frame instead of polars.

Returns

One row per (season, team_id, coach_id): coach_id is the stats.nba.com coach id, team the team abbreviation, role "HC". team_games is the team's completed games of season_type, games / wins / losses / win_pct / pts / opp_pts the coach's (final scores), first_game_date / last_game_date his span. pbp_games, poss, opp_poss, poss_pts, opp_poss_pts, seconds and the o_* / d_* four-factor counts come from his games' possessions, and give off_rtg, def_rtg, net_rtg, pace and efg_pct .. opp_ft_rate. win_pct and the twelve rates have <m>_rank (1 = best), <m>_pct and <m>_n, within the season among coaches with half the most games; null under five coaches. Empty input returns that schema with zero rows.

nba_cup_sim​

nba_cup_sim(season: 'int', *, as_of_date: 'dt.date | None' = None, n_sims: 'int' = 10000, seed: 'int' = 20261103, cup_schedule: 'pl.DataFrame | None' = None, return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

NBA Cup sim as of a date: odds to win the group, take a wild card, reach each round and win.

Runs the season sim (sportsdataverse.nba.nba_season_sim.nba_season_sim: the as-of possession ridge with the preseason prior, one rating shift per team per simulated season, the same seed and draws) with the Cup inside it, ranks every simulated group with the Cup tiebreakers (nba_cup_standings), and plays the knockout: seed 1 hosts the wild card and seed 2 seed 3 in each conference, the semifinals neutral through 2025-26 and at the higher seed from 2026-27, the final neutral. A knockout game already played keeps its result.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
seasonintThe season, named by the year it ends (2027 is 2026-27); from 2024.
as_of_datedate | NoneNoneGames on or after this date are simulated even when they have a score (a backtest). None: every final game counts as played.
n_simsint10000Simulated Cups (Monte Carlo error: the _se columns).
seedint20261103Seeds the generator; the same data and seed give the same output.
cup_scheduleDataFrame | NoneNoneA ~sportsdataverse.nba.nba_stats.nba_stats_scheduleleaguev2 frame; None fetches it from stats.nba.com (residential connection).
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas frame instead of polars.

Returns

One row per team (team_id, Int64), sorted by conference, group and mean group rank: the Group Play wins / losses / point_diff so far, the as-of adj_net_rtg, p_win_group, p_wild_card, p_quarterfinal (either), p_semifinal, p_final, p_champion, each with its Monte Carlo _se (sqrt(p (1 - p) / n_sims)), mean_group_rank and n_sims.

col_nametypedescription
seasonInt64Season, named by the year it ends (2027 is 2026-27).
as_of_dateDateThe as-of date: games on or after it are simulated. Null when every final game counts as played.
conferenceStringNBA conference (East / West).
cup_groupStringThe team's NBA Cup group (East Group A .. West Group C).
team_idInt64stats.nba.com team id (Int64).
winsInt64Group Play wins in the games already played.
lossesInt64Group Play losses in the games already played.
point_diffInt64Group Play point differential in the games already played, 0 for a game that went to overtime.
adj_net_rtgFloat64As-of adjusted net rating from the season sim's possession ridge: points per 100 over an average opponent on a neutral court.
p_win_groupFloat64Share of simulated Cups in which the team wins its group under the Cup tiebreakers.
p_wild_cardFloat64Share of simulated Cups in which the team takes its conference's wild card: the best second-place team.
p_quarterfinalFloat64Share of simulated Cups in which the team reaches the knockout: a group win or the wild card.
p_semifinalFloat64Share of simulated Cups in which the team wins its quarterfinal.
p_finalFloat64Share of simulated Cups in which the team reaches the final.
p_championFloat64Share of simulated Cups the team wins (the final included).
p_win_group_seFloat64Monte Carlo standard error of p_win_group: sqrt(p (1 - p) / n_sims).
p_wild_card_seFloat64Monte Carlo standard error of p_wild_card: sqrt(p (1 - p) / n_sims).
p_quarterfinal_seFloat64Monte Carlo standard error of p_quarterfinal: sqrt(p (1 - p) / n_sims).
p_semifinal_seFloat64Monte Carlo standard error of p_semifinal: sqrt(p (1 - p) / n_sims).
p_final_seFloat64Monte Carlo standard error of p_final: sqrt(p (1 - p) / n_sims).
p_champion_seFloat64Monte Carlo standard error of p_champion: sqrt(p (1 - p) / n_sims).
mean_group_rankFloat64Mean Group Play finish (1-5) over the simulated Cups.
n_simsInt64Number of simulated Cups behind every share on the row.

Raises

ExceptionDescription
ValueErrorIf season is before 2024 or n_sims < 1.

Example

import datetime as dt
from sportsdataverse.nba.nba_cup import nba_cup_sim
nba_cup_sim(2027, as_of_date=dt.date(2026, 11, 6)).select("team_id", "p_quarterfinal", "p_champion")

nba_cup_standings​

nba_cup_standings(season: 'int', *, as_of_date: 'dt.date | None' = None, cup_schedule: 'pl.DataFrame | None' = None, seed: 'int' = 20261103, return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

NBA Cup Group Play standings as of a date, with the official tiebreak order and wild cards.

The rules (NBA.com, "Emirates NBA Cup 101", https://www.nba.com/news/nba-cup-101, read 2026-11-04) are in sportsdataverse.nba.nba_cup: Group Play record, then head-to-head, point differential and points scored in Group Play (overtime excluded), the previous regular-season record and a random drawing; the wild card is the best second-place team of each conference by the same order without head-to-head.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
seasonintThe season, named by the year it ends (2027 is 2026-27); from 2024.
as_of_datedate | NoneNoneGames on or after this date are unplayed. None: every final game counts.
cup_scheduleDataFrame | NoneNoneA ~sportsdataverse.nba.nba_stats.nba_stats_scheduleleaguev2 frame for the season. None fetches it from stats.nba.com (a residential connection: the site hangs on datacenter IPs).
seedint20261103Seeds the random drawing, the last tiebreaker.
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas frame instead of polars.

Returns

One row per team (stats.nba.com team_id, Int64), sorted by conference, group and rank: group_rank with wins / losses / games_remaining, the tiebreak inputs point_diff / points (overtime excluded) / prior_win_pct, tiebreak (the criterion that placed the team among those with its record; null when the record alone did), conference_rank (the group leaders 1-3, then the rest, the wild-card race), leads_group, holds_wild_card and knockout_seed (1-4 on the current table).

col_nametypedescription
seasonInt64Season, named by the year it ends (2027 is 2026-27).
as_of_dateDateThe as-of date: games on or after it are not counted. Null when every final game counts.
conferenceStringNBA conference (East / West).
cup_groupStringThe team's NBA Cup group (East Group A .. West Group C).
team_idInt64stats.nba.com team id (Int64).
group_rankInt64Place in the group (1-5) by Group Play record, then head-to-head, point differential, points scored, the previous season's record and a random drawing.
winsInt64Group Play wins.
lossesInt64Group Play losses.
games_remainingInt64Group Play games still to play.
point_diffInt64Group Play point differential, 0 for a game that went to overtime (the Cup rule).
pointsInt64Group Play points scored, overtime points excluded (the Cup rule).
prior_win_pctFloat64The previous regular season's winning percentage, the fourth tiebreaker.
tiebreakStringThe criterion that placed the team among the teams with its group record: head_to_head, point_diff, points, prior_record or random; null when the record alone did.
conference_rankInt64Rank in the conference as stats.nba.com orders it: the group leaders 1-3 (the seeding order), then the second-place teams (the wild-card race), then the rest, each by record and the tiebreakers without head-to-head.
leads_groupBooleanWhether the team leads (or won) its group.
holds_wild_cardBooleanWhether the team holds its conference's wild card on the current table (the best second-place team).
knockout_seedInt64Knockout seed (1-3 the group leaders by record, 4 the wild card) on the current table; null otherwise.

Raises

ExceptionDescription
ValueErrorIf season is before 2024 or the schedule does not hold six groups of five.

Example

import polars as pl
from sportsdataverse.nba.nba_cup import nba_cup_standings
nba_cup_standings(2026).filter(pl.col("knockout_seed").is_not_null())

nba_draft_lottery_odds(*, league_id: 'str' = '00', chances: 'Sequence[float] | None' = None, return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

Exact draft-lottery odds by pre-lottery slot: the chance each slot gets each pick.

NBA: the 2019 odds, 14 slots, four picks drawn. WNBA: two picks drawn, with the published chances for the number of lottery teams. Every ordered draw is enumerated (24,024 for the NBA), so the odds are exact, not simulated.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
league_idstr'00'"00" NBA or "10" WNBA.
chancesSequence[float] | NoneNoneChances per slot, worst record first. Default: NBA_LOTTERY_CHANCES (NBA) or the five-team WNBA_LOTTERY_CHANCES table (WNBA, the 2026 lottery).
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas frame instead of polars.

Returns

One row per (lottery_slot, pick): lottery_slot (1 = the worst record), chances (the slot's chances), pick and probability.

col_nametypedescription
lottery_slotInt64Pre-lottery slot, 1 = the worst record.
chancesFloat64The slot's chances in 1,000 (NBA 2019 table; WNBA published table or the caller's).
pickInt64Draft pick number.
probabilityFloat64Exact probability that the slot gets the pick, from enumerating every ordered draw.

Raises

ExceptionDescription
ValueErrorIf league_id is not "00" or "10", or chances has a non-positive or non-finite entry or fewer entries than picks drawn.

Example

import polars as pl
from sportsdataverse.nba.nba_season_rules import nba_draft_lottery_odds

odds.filter(pl.col("lottery_slot") == 1)

# A WNBA lottery with its own chances

nba_foul_drawing(season: 'str', *, league_id: 'str' = '00', base: 'Optional[pl.DataFrame]' = None, advanced: 'Optional[pl.DataFrame]' = None, player_mix: 'Optional[pl.DataFrame]' = None, return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'Union[pl.DataFrame, pd.DataFrame]'

Expected FTA + residual foul-drawing skill from Synergy play-type mix.

lg_ft_rate_t = poss-weighted league mean of ft_freq for play type t (Σ_players(ft_freq_t·poss_t) / Σ_players(poss_t)). expected_fta = scale · Σ_t poss_t · lg_ft_rate_t; foul_draw_skill = 100·(fta − expected_fta)/poss.

ft_freq (Synergy's ft_poss_pct) counts FT-drawing trips, not individual free-throw attempts (a shooting foul is typically a 2-shot trip) -- scale = Σ actual fta / Σ raw trip-based estimate is derived from the fetched season itself (never hard-coded) so the trip-to-attempt conversion self-normalizes and Σ expected_fta ≡ Σ fta holds exactly.

league_idstr'00'"00" NBA (default), "10" WNBA, "20" G-League.

advancedOptional[DataFrame]NoneInjected Advanced-measure frame with player_id, pfd (personal fouls drawn); optional -- pfd is null when omitted (fta is the always-present proxy).
player_mixOptional[DataFrame]NoneInjected Synergy player-level offensive mix: player_id, play_type, poss, ft_freq.
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas DataFrame instead of polars.

Returns

One row per player: player_id (Int64), poss/fta/ expected_fta/foul_draw_skill (Float64), pfd (Float64, null when advanced has no data for that player or is omitted). Zero-row frame with this schema when the inputs are empty (sparse-coverage leagues never raise).

Raises

ExceptionDescription
ValueErrorgame_id is not one non-negative integer id -- a bool, a negative or fractional number, a string that is not all digits, or more than 10 digits (checked before any request).
NoDataErrorThe game has no L2M report (a 404, or S3's AccessDenied 403). Only games within 3 points (5 before 2017-18) at some point in the last two minutes of the fourth quarter or overtime get one, so most games have none; a game that just ended may not be graded yet.

nba_l2m_games​

nba_l2m_games(season: 'int | str', *, return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False, proxy: 'dict | None' = None) -> 'Union[pl.DataFrame, pd.DataFrame]'

Fetch the list of games with Last Two Minute reports for an NBA season.

Retrieves and parses the L2M season index page from official.nba.com, returning a table of all games for which L2M reports exist. JSON reports exist only from 2019-01-01 onward; earlier seasons' index pages list PDFs, which this function ignores. A release loader for historical (PDF-era) reports is planned but does not exist yet.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
seasonint | strThe NBA season (end year) as a 4-digit int or string, e.g. 2026 for the 2025-26 season.
return_as_pandasboolFalseIf True, return a pandas DataFrame instead of polars.
proxydict | NoneNoneOptional proxy dict passed through to the HTTP layer.

Returns

A polars.DataFrame (or pandas.DataFrame when return_as_pandas=True) with schema {"game_id": Utf8, "season": Int32, "season_type": Utf8, "label": Utf8}, one row per unique game ID in page order.

col_nametypedescription
game_idString10-digit NBA game id (zero-padded), parsed from the season listing page's report link.
seasonInt64NBA season end year passed to the function (e.g. 2026 for the 2025-26 season), stamped onto every row.
season_typeStringSeason type inferred from the third digit of game_id: preseason, regular, all-star, playoffs, play-in, or nba-cup-final.
labelStringMatchup label text scraped from the listing page link, e.g. "Thunder 125, Rockets 124 (2OT)".

Raises

ExceptionDescription
ValueErrorseason is not a 4-digit year (an int or an all-digit string), checked before any request.
NoDataErrorOfficial.nba.com has no listing page for that season (a 404, or S3's AccessDenied 403) -- e.g. a future season, or one from before the L2M program began.
AssetFetchErrorThe fetch failed (network error, rate limit, or Akamai WAF block), or a 200 response that is missing the expected "Last Two Minute" page marker (an Akamai interstitial, a blank body, or a redesigned page), or that links reports in a shape the parser does not read (a redesigned page) -- checked here, not in parse_nba_l2m_games, so the parser itself never raises.

Example

from sportsdataverse.nba.nba_officiating import nba_l2m_games

print(f"Season had {df.height} games with L2M reports")

# Get playoff games only

df = nba_l2m_games(2026)
playoffs = df.filter(df["season_type"] == "playoffs")

# Convert to pandas

df = nba_l2m_games(2026, return_as_pandas=True)

nba_lineup_count_rows​

nba_lineup_count_rows(possessions: 'pl.DataFrame', schedule: 'pl.DataFrame') -> 'pl.DataFrame'

Map nba_stats_possessions rows onto the lineup-seasons count frame.

Each possession's counts go to the side that had the ball, its dreb (the defense's defensive rebounds) to the other side, on the possession's lineup. A possession whose offense is neither team of its schedule game is dropped, as in the game impact box.
