nba_player_shot_zones(seasons: 'int | list[int]', *, league_id: 'str' = '00', return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

Season shot profile by zone per player: rim / mid / corner 3 / above-the-break 3.

One row per player, team and season over the regular season (a traded player has a row per team). Zones and the unlocated attempts are as in the module docstring.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription

return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas frame instead of polars.

Returns

Every rate has <m>_n (its denominator), <m>_rank (1 = highest) and <m>_pct (the Weibull percentile), ranked within the season among players whose <m>_n reaches the rate's gate and null under ten players. nba_player_war​

nba_player_war(seasons: 'int | list[int]', *, league_id: 'str' = '00', return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

WAR and VORP per player-season, from our BPM, RAPM and BOBAN (regular season).

Each rating is anchored to the team (its players' possession-weighted mean rating over the season is the team's net rating / 5; it is a season constraint, not an estimate for each five-man lineup) and centred on the league, so 0 is a league-average player. vorp is points per 100 team possessions above a replacement player over one team's season of possessions (a full season of possessions at R + 1 per 100 is a VORP of 1); war is wins above replacement. A replacement-level player has 0, and a season's war adds up to the league's wins minus those of a team of replacement players. rating / war are the mean of the three sources; rating_<s> / war_<s> are each one. The replacement level and points per win are fitted per league (~sportsdataverse._common.player_war.REPLACEMENT_LEVEL, ~sportsdataverse._common.player_war.PTS_PER_WIN).

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
seasonsint | list[int]A season or list of seasons, named by the year it ends (2026 is 2025-26). The WNBA season is its calendar year.
league_idstr'00'"00" NBA or "10" WNBA.
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas frame instead of polars.

Returns

One row per (season, player_id) (stats.nba.com ids, Int64): player_name, team_id (the team he played most possessions for), teams (how many he played for), min, poss (on-court possessions, offensive plus defensive over 2), rating and rating_bpm / rating_rapm / rating_boban (anchored, per 100), vorp, war with war_lo / war_hi (a nominal 90% interval from a bootstrap over each team-season's games: the sampling uncertainty of the team side and the playing time) and war_bpm / war_rapm / war_boban (the source spread), with _rank (1 = most), _pct and _n (possessions) for vorp and war among every player of the season. Sorted by season and war. Empty input returns that schema with zero rows.

nba_rated_priors(season: 'int', *, league_id: 'str' = '00', return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

Rated pregame home win probability for every ESPN game of a season, as of each game's date.

The in-game win-probability prior when a game has no betting line (~sportsdataverse.nba.nba_game_page.home_prior's rated_home_prob); see the module docstring for the model and the as-of boundary.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
seasonintThe season, named by the year it ends (2026 is 2025-26); the WNBA season is its calendar year.
league_idstr'00'"00" NBA or "10" WNBA.
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas frame instead of polars.

Returns

One row per game of the season in the ESPN schedule release (scheduled games included; a row the release labels with another season is dropped): season, game_id, game_date, home_team_id, away_team_id (ESPN ids, Int32, as in the pbp release), neutral_site, stats_game_id, the two teams' adjusted net ratings before the game (home_adj_net_rtg, away_adj_net_rtg, per 100), the home term hfa (per 100, applied to both offenses), exp_home_margin (points) and rated_home_prob. The last six are null for a game with no stats.nba.com match (All-Star games and the WNBA Commissioner's Cup final).

col_nametypedescription
seasonInt64Season, named by the year it ends (the WNBA season is its calendar year).
game_idInt64ESPN game id (Int32, as in the pbp release).
game_dateDateGame date (ESPN schedule); the ratings use the games dated strictly before it.
home_team_idInt64ESPN home team id.
away_team_idInt64ESPN away team id.
neutral_siteBooleanESPN's neutral-site flag; a neutral game gets no home term.
stats_game_idStringThe stats.nba.com / stats.wnba.com game id matched on date and team names; null for a game with no match (All-Star games).
home_adj_net_rtgFloat64Home team's adjusted net rating before the game, points per 100 over an average opponent on a neutral court (the season sim's as-of possession ridge, shrunk toward last season's regressed rating).
away_adj_net_rtgFloat64Away team's adjusted net rating before the game, points per 100, on the same basis.
hfaFloat64The as-of ridge's home term, points per 100 for the home offense over a neutral court (applied to both offenses).
exp_home_marginFloat64Expected home margin in points: (home_adj_net_rtg - away_adj_net_rtg + 2 * hfa) * possessions per game / 100, without the home term at a neutral site.
rated_home_probFloat64Rated pregame home win probability: Phi(exp_home_margin / margin_sd) with the season sim's probit scale. Pass it to score_win_prob / game_page_tables as rated_home_prob to make it the prior of a game with no line; without it that game gets home-court advantage (0.5 at a neutral site).

ValueErrorIf league_id is not "00" or "10".

nba_ratings_panel​

nba_ratings_panel(model: 'AnyModel', possessions: 'pl.DataFrame', dates: 'Optional[Sequence[datetime.date]]' = None, *, return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'Union[pl.DataFrame, pd.DataFrame]'

Player-ratings-through-date long panel: one row per (player_id, date).

Refit-per-checkpoint (v1; no warm-start incrementality) — each date's row calls ratings_as_of independently, so the panel is leakage-free by construction: a possession dated after a given checkpoint can never affect that checkpoint's row, no matter what other dates are also being computed or what future rows exist in possessions. Cost is a full refit per checkpoint date; for a season's sparse RAPM-family design this is seconds per date, not minutes — acceptable for a nightly/daily cadence but not for live in-game updating (out of scope; see spec non-goals).

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
modelAnyModelA harness model conforming to nba_model_validation.AnyModel.
possessionsDataFrameA possession+lineup frame with a game_date (pl.Date) column (as emitted by compile_nba_season).
datesOptional[Sequence[date]]NoneCheckpoint dates to compute. None (default) uses every distinct game_date present in possessions, sorted ascending — a rating for every game day, matching what EPM/LEBRON publish nightly. Duplicates are deduped; input order does not matter (the output is always sorted by date).
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn pandas instead of polars.

Returns

Long frame with RATINGS_PANEL_SCHEMA columns (player_id, date, o_rating, d_rating, rating). Zero-row (that schema) when possessions is empty or no date yields any players.

No returns table is published for this function: no capture: its possessions input needs a game_date column, and neither nba_possessions nor the released possessions carry one.

Raises

ExceptionDescription
ValueErrorIf possessions (non-empty) lacks a game_date column.

Example

import datetime
from sportsdataverse.nba.nba_model_validation import RidgeRapmModel
from sportsdataverse.nba.nba_ratings_panel import nba_ratings_panel

checkpoints = [datetime.date(2023, 11, 1), datetime.date(2023, 12, 1)]
panel = nba_ratings_panel(RidgeRapmModel(), season_poss, dates=checkpoints)
print(panel.filter(pl.col("player_id") == 201939).sort("date"))

# Every game day, no explicit grid

panel = nba_ratings_panel(RidgeRapmModel(), season_poss)

nba_raw_store_season_frame​

nba_raw_store_season_frame(endpoint: 'str', season: 'int', variant: 'Optional[str]' = None, *, result_set: 'Optional[str]' = None, raw_store_dir: 'RawStoreDir' = None) -> "Optional['pl.DataFrame']"

Read a committed SEASON-LEVEL capture from the raw store, parsed to a frame.

The per-game half of the store is served by the read-through per-game path; this is the season-keyed half (leaguegamelog, playerindex, leaguedashplayerbiostats, ...) that the -raw scraper writes as

  • {endpoint}/{season}/{variant}.json -- parameterized captures, where variant is the slugified parameter sweep (e.g. "regular-season", "regular-season_totals"), and
  • {endpoint}/{season}.json -- unparameterized captures (e.g. playerindex), i.e. variant=None.

Roots may be a local checkout or an http(s):// base (the raw repo served over raw.githubusercontent / a CDN), so a consumer -- notably the hoopR-nba-stats-data model producer -- runs clone-free in CI against the same committed tree the per-game compile reads.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
endpointstrstats.nba.com endpoint slug (the store subdirectory).
seasonintSeason END year (2024 = 2023-24) -- the key both halves of the store are filed under.
variantOptional[str]NoneCapture variant slug, or None for an unparameterized capture.
result_setOptional[str]NoneNamed result set to return when the payload carries several; defaults to the first frame that parses.
raw_store_dirRawStoreDirNoneStore root spec (dir or URL base) or per-endpoint mapping; None falls back to the env vars, "" disables.

Returns

The parsed polars.DataFrame, or None when the store is unset, the capture is absent, or the payload carries no usable frame -- so a caller can cleanly fall back to a live fetch.
