nba_team_shot_zones​

nba_team_shot_zones(seasons: 'int | list[int]', *, league_id: 'str' = '00', return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

Season shot profile by zone per team, for and allowed: rim / mid / corner 3 / above-the-break 3.

One row per team and season over the regular season: the team's own attempts and, with an opp_ prefix, its opponents' attempts against it.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
seasonsint | list[int]A season or list of seasons, named by the year it ends (2026 is 2025-26). The WNBA season is its calendar year.
league_idstr'00'"00" NBA or "10" WNBA.
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas frame instead of polars.

Returns

One row per (season, team_id) (the stats.nba.com team id, Int64) with games and the columns of nba_player_shot_zones for the team and again with an opp_ prefix for its opponents (opp_fga, opp_rim_fg_pct, ...), each rate with <m>_n, <m>_rank and <m>_pct, ranked within the season among teams whose <m>_n reaches the gate and null under five teams. <m>_rank is 1 = highest, except the allowed opp_{z}_fg_pct, where 1 is the lowest (the stingiest defense). Empty input returns that schema with zero rows.

nba_team_strength​

nba_team_strength(seasons: 'int | list[int]', *, league_id: 'str' = '00', season_type: 'str' = 'regular-season', return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

Team strength from possessions: adjusted ratings, SOS, SOV, pace and the four factors.

One row per team and season. The adjusted ratings are a ridge regression of points per 100 on each possession on offense, defense and home terms (penalty ~sportsdataverse._common.team_strength.RIDGE_LAMBDA, fit by season-disjoint CV): points scored / allowed per 100 against an average opponent on a neutral court. SOS is the mean opponent adj_net_rtg over the team's games, SOV the same over its wins.

player_play_context​

player_play_context(possessions: 'pl.DataFrame', *, league_non_transition_ppp: 'Optional[float]' = None, apply_ctg_filters: 'bool' = True, return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'Union[pl.DataFrame, pd.DataFrame]'

Per-player offensive On/Off Play-Context table (CTG's On/Off page, offense half).

For each player: their team's offensive play-context with them on the floor (on_*), without them (off_*), and the on-minus-off difference (diff_*) — which is the number CTG actually displays.

The OFF side is derived by subtraction (team total minus on-court), not by a second scan. That is deliberate: it makes the partition exact by construction — on_poss + off_poss == team_poss and the same for points — so a leak (a double-counted possession, a dropped lineup slot) is impossible to hide. The test suite asserts that identity directly.

Like CTG's on/off, this is a raw split: no luck adjustment, no opponent adjustment, no minutes threshold. It is a descriptive difference, not a causal estimate — for that, use the RAPM surface (~sportsdataverse.nba.nba_rapm.nba_rapm).

player_rates(box_logs: 'pl.DataFrame') -> 'pl.DataFrame'

Per-player per-minute rate stats from box logs.

Rows with null, zero or negative minutes (DNPs) are dropped before anything is counted, so games counts only games the player logged minutes in.
