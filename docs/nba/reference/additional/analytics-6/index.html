nba_situational_team_games​

nba_situational_team_games(seasons: 'int | list[int]', *, league_id: 'str' = '00', season_type: 'str' = 'regular-season', top_n: 'int | None' = None, return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

Team splits by situation, one game at a time: nba_situational_team per game.

The same possessions and labels, grouped by game too, so a team-season's rows of a split add up to its nba_situational_team row (games, seconds, poss, opp_poss, pts, opp_pts). The opponent family keeps the season's top top_n.

return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas frame instead of polars.

Returns

One row per (season, team_id, game_id, family, split), the columns of nba_situational_team plus game_id (the stats.nba.com game id); games is 1. A split the team did not play in the game has no row. Empty input returns that schema with zero rows.

nba_team_game_four_factors​

nba_team_game_four_factors(seasons: 'int | list[int]', *, league_id: 'str' = '00', season_type: 'str' = 'regular-season', return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

Per team and game: the four factors in points against the season's league-average offense.

pts_vs_exp (points less a league-average offense on the same counted possessions) split exactly into shooting_pts, turnover_pts, rebounding_pts, free_throw_pts and residual_pts, the possession count's distance from the box estimate beyond the league's. The prices and the residual's formula are in sportsdataverse._common.team_season_rates.team_game_four_factors; every league value is the season's (the same possessions as nba_team_season_rates) and is on each row.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
seasonsint | list[int]A season or list of seasons, named by the year it ends (2026 is 2025-26). The WNBA season is its calendar year.
league_idstr'00'"00" NBA or "10" WNBA.
season_typestr'regular-season'"regular-season" (default), "preseason", "playoffs" or "play-in", matched to the schedule's season_type. The league values are that season type's.
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas frame instead of polars.

Returns

One row per (season, team_id, game_id). team_id is the stats.nba.com team id (Int64), game_id the stats.nba.com game id. poss / pts are the team's counted possessions and points, exp_pts is lg_ppp * poss and pts_vs_exp the difference; the five *_pts columns add up to it (they are null when a league value they use has a zero denominator). lg_ppp, lg_efg_pct, lg_tov_pct, lg_orb_pct, lg_ft_rate, lg_ft_pct and chance_pts are the season's league values the factors are priced with. Empty input returns that schema with zero rows.

col_nametypedescription
seasonInt64Season, named by the year it ends (2026 is 2025-26).
team_idInt64stats.nba.com team id (the 10-digit franchise id).
game_idStringstats.nba.com game id.
possInt64The team's offensive possessions in the game, counted from possession rows.
ptsInt64Points scored on the team's offensive possessions in the game.
exp_ptsFloat64Points a league-average offense scores on the team's counted possessions, lg_ppp x poss.
pts_vs_expFloat64Points above (+) or below (-) a league-average offense on the same possessions, pts - exp_pts. The four *_pts factors and residual_pts add up to it exactly; they are null (and do not) when a league value they use has a zero denominator or the game has no counts.
shooting_ptsFloat64Points from shooting: 2 x (eFG% - lg_efg_pct) x FGA, the same shots above a league-average shooter, plus chance_pts x lg_orb_pct x the rebound chances above the league's per FGA: the misses a league-average offense gets back.
turnover_ptsFloat64Points from turnovers: (lg_tov_pct x poss - TOV) x chance_pts, the turnovers saved, each a chance.
rebounding_ptsFloat64Points from offensive rebounding: (OREB - lg_orb_pct x (OREB + opponent DREB)) x chance_pts, the extra offensive rebounds on the team's own rebound chances, each a new chance.
free_throw_ptsFloat64Points from free throws: (FTA - lg_ft_rate x FGA) x (lg_ft_pct - 0.44 x chance_pts), the extra free throws at a league-average make less the share of a chance each uses, plus FTM - lg_ft_pct x FTA, the makes above league FT%.
residual_ptsFloat64The remainder, exactly chance_pts x (box_poss - poss - FGA x the league's box_poss - poss per FGA), box_poss = FGA - OREB + TOV + 0.44 FTA: the counted possessions' distance from the box estimate beyond the league's. Sums to zero over the league.
lg_pppFloat64League points per counted possession in the season, over the priced team-games (those with all their counts).
lg_efg_pctFloat64League effective field-goal percentage in the season, (FGM + 0.5 3PM) / FGA over the priced team-games (those with all their counts).
lg_tov_pctFloat64League turnovers per counted possession in the season, over the priced team-games (those with all their counts).
lg_orb_pctFloat64League offensive-rebound percentage in the season, OREB / (OREB + opponent DREB) over the priced team-games (those with all their counts).
lg_ft_rateFloat64League free-throw rate in the season, FTA / FGA over the priced team-games (those with all their counts).
lg_ft_pctFloat64League free-throw percentage in the season, FTM / FTA over the priced team-games (those with all their counts).
chance_ptsFloat64Points a chance is worth, lg_ppp / (1 - lg_tov_pct): a possession that does not end in a turnover. The price of a turnover saved and of an extra offensive rebound.

Raises

ExceptionDescription
ValueErrorIf league_id is not "00" or "10", or season_type is not one of the four above.

Example

from sportsdataverse.nba.nba_team_season_rates import nba_team_game_four_factors
books = nba_team_game_four_factors(2026)
books.filter(team_id=1610612738).select("game_id", "pts_vs_exp", "shooting_pts")

nba_team_game_possessions​

nba_team_game_possessions(seasons: 'int | list[int]', *, league_id: 'str' = '00', season_type: 'str' = 'regular-season', return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

Per team and game: the possession counts behind nba_team_season_rates.

The season table's poss / opp_poss / pts / opp_pts one game at a time, from the same possessions, so a team-season's rows of a split add up to its season row exactly (their count is games). Rate any set of games the same way: sum the rows, then divide once (off_rtg = 100 * pts / poss; pace 48 * 60 * (poss + opp_poss) / 2 / seconds, 40 in the WNBA). The definitions are in sportsdataverse._common.team_season_rates.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
seasonsint | list[int]A season or list of seasons, named by the year it ends (2026 is 2025-26). The WNBA season is its calendar year.
league_idstr'00'"00" NBA or "10" WNBA.
season_typestr'regular-season'"regular-season" (default), "preseason", "playoffs" or "play-in", matched to the schedule's season_type.
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas frame instead of polars.

Returns

One row per (season, team_id, game_id, split): a game has an all row, a home or away row (neither at a neutral site) and, when it has clutch possessions, close and clutch rows. team_id is the stats.nba.com team id (Int64), game_id the stats.nba.com game id. poss / opp_poss are the team's offensive / defensive possessions, pts / opp_pts their points and seconds the game clock they used. Empty input returns that schema with zero rows.

col_nametypedescription
seasonInt64Season, named by the year it ends (2026 is 2025-26).
team_idInt64stats.nba.com team id (the 10-digit franchise id).
game_idStringstats.nba.com game id. The game this row's possessions are from.
splitStringThe season table's split this game is in: all (every game); home or away (neither at a neutral site); close and clutch when the game has clutch possessions. A team-season's rows of a split add up to its nba_team_season_rates row.
possInt64Offensive possessions in the split, counted from possession rows.
opp_possInt64Defensive possessions in the split (the opponents' offensive possessions).
ptsInt64Points scored on the team's offensive possessions in the split. Counted from each possession's made shots and free throws.
opp_ptsInt64Points allowed on the team's defensive possessions in the split.
secondsFloat64Game-clock seconds the team's possessions in the split used; pace over a set of games is regulation minutes x 60 x (poss + opp_poss) / 2 / summed seconds.

Raises

ExceptionDescription
ValueErrorIf league_id is not "00" or "10", or season_type is not one of the four above.

Example

import polars as pl
from sportsdataverse.nba.nba_team_season_rates import nba_team_game_possessions
games = nba_team_game_possessions(2026).filter(split="all")
games.group_by("team_id").agg(pl.col("pts").sum() * 100 / pl.col("poss").sum())

nba_team_luck​

nba_team_luck(seasons: 'int | list[int]', *, league_id: 'str' = '00', season_type: 'str' = 'regular-season', return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

Luck and close-game record: Pythagorean expected wins vs actual wins, and close-game W-L.

One row per team and season. exp_wins is the Pythagorean expectation on the season's final scores, games * pts^x / (pts^x + opp_pts^x), with the exponent x fitted per league on past seasons (~sportsdataverse._common.team_luck.PYTHAG_EXPONENT). luck_wins is wins - exp_wins. A close game is one within 5 points at the start of a possession in play in the last 5:00 of the 4th quarter or in overtime: one that starts there, or starts before 5:00 and ends after it (stats.nba.com's clutch window; BB-D3's close split). For the WNBA (league_id="10") through 2005, two 20-minute halves, the regulation window is the last 5:00 of the 2nd half; overtime counts as above.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
seasonsint | list[int]A season or list of seasons, named by the year it ends (2026 is 2025-26). The WNBA season is its calendar year.
league_idstr'00'"00" NBA or "10" WNBA.
season_typestr'regular-season'"regular-season" (default), "preseason", "playoffs" or "play-in", matched to the schedule's season_type. The exponent was fitted on regular seasons; on a handful of playoff or play-in games exp_wins is only indicative.
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas frame instead of polars.

Returns

One row per (season, team_id); team_id is the stats.nba.com team id (Int64). games, wins, losses, pts and opp_pts are from the final scores; win_pct, pythag_win_pct, exp_wins and luck_wins (wins - exp_wins) follow. pbp_games counts the games with published possessions, and close_games, close_wins, close_losses and close_win_pct (null with no close game) are the close games among them. pythag_win_pct, luck_wins and close_win_pct have <m>_rank (1 = highest), <m>_pct (the Weibull percentile) and <m>_n (games, or close games); ranks are within the season, among teams with at least half the most games (close_win_pct also needs possessions for half of them and five close games), and null under five teams. Empty input returns that schema with zero rows.

col_nametypedescription
seasonInt64Season, named by the year it ends (2026 is 2025-26).
team_idInt64stats.nba.com team id (the 10-digit franchise id).
gamesInt64Games in the season type's completed games (the stats schedule's final scores).
winsInt64Wins in the season type's completed games (the stats schedule's final scores).
lossesInt64Losses in the season type's completed games (the stats schedule's final scores).
ptsInt64Points scored in those games (final scores).
opp_ptsInt64Points allowed in those games (final scores).

close_win_pct_rankFloat64Rank of close_win_pct within the season (the highest value ranks 1; ties average), among teams that played at least half the most games any team played in the season, have possessions for at least half their games and played at least five close games (MIN_CLOSE_GAMES); null otherwise, for a null value, or with fewer than five qualifiers, or when every qualifier has the same value.
pythag_win_pct_pctFloat64Percentile of pythag_win_pct within the season among the same qualified teams: 100 * (n + 1 - rank) / (n + 1), n the number ranked, so the best team is under 100 and nobody is 0; null when the rank is null.
luck_wins_pctFloat64Percentile of luck_wins within the season among the same qualified teams: 100 * (n + 1 - rank) / (n + 1), n the number ranked, so the best team is under 100 and nobody is 0; null when the rank is null.
close_win_pct_pctFloat64Percentile of close_win_pct within the season among the same qualified teams: 100 * (n + 1 - rank) / (n + 1), n the number ranked, so the best team is under 100 and nobody is 0; null when the rank is null.
pythag_win_pct_nInt64Sample size of pythag_win_pct: games.
luck_wins_nInt64Sample size of luck_wins: games.
close_win_pct_nInt64Sample size of close_win_pct: close games.

Raises

ValueErrorIf league_id is not "00" or "10", or season_type is not one of the four above (checked before any data is loaded).
