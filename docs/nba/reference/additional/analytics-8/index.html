nba_team_strength​

nba_team_strength(seasons: 'int | list[int]', *, league_id: 'str' = '00', season_type: 'str' = 'regular-season', return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

Team strength from possessions: adjusted ratings, SOS, SOV, pace and the four factors.

One row per team and season. The adjusted ratings are a ridge regression of points per 100 on each possession on offense, defense and home terms (penalty ~sportsdataverse._common.team_strength.RIDGE_LAMBDA, fit by season-disjoint CV): points scored / allowed per 100 against an average opponent on a neutral court. SOS is the mean opponent adj_net_rtg over the team's games, SOV the same over its wins.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
seasonsint | list[int]A season or list of seasons, named by the year it ends (2026 is 2025-26). The WNBA season is its calendar year.
league_idstr'00'"00" NBA or "10" WNBA.
season_typestr'regular-season'"regular-season" (default), "preseason", "playoffs" or "play-in", matched to the schedule's season_type. The ridge, the rates and the games all use it.
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas frame instead of polars.

Returns

One row per (season, team_id); team_id is the stats.nba.com team id (Int64). games, poss / opp_poss, pts / opp_pts are the season totals from the possessions; wins / losses come from the schedule's final scores. The rated metrics are adj_off_rtg, adj_def_rtg, adj_net_rtg, sos, sov and the BB-D3 rates (off_rtg, def_rtg, net_rtg, pace, the four factors and the four against). Each has <m>_rank (1 = best; adj_def_rtg, def_rtg, tov_pct and the opponent shooting, rebounding and free-throw rates rank low first; sos ranks the hardest schedule first), <m>_pct (the Weibull percentile) and <m>_n (possessions for the ratings, completed games against a rated opponent for SOS, the clock in regulation games for pace, wins over a rated opponent for SOV). Ranks are within the season, among teams with at least half the most games, the BB-D3 rates' also behind their ~sportsdataverse._common.rate_gates.TEAM_MIN_N gate, and null under five teams. Empty input returns that schema with zero rows.

Drive value over expected + rim-pressure, per player-season.

prop_distribution​

prop_distribution(exp_value: 'float', stat: 'str', *, league_id: 'str' = '00', recent_value: 'float | None' = None, recent_weight: 'float | None' = None) -> 'tuple[str, dict[str, Any]]'

Distribution family + parameters for a projected stat mean.

Points -> Normal (mu, sd) with sd = a + b*sqrt(mu); counts (reb/ast/fg3m, and stl/blk/tov/fgm/ftm where the league has their fit) -> Negative-Binomial (r, p) matching mean mu and variance dispersion*mu (Poisson if dispersion <= 1). With a positive recent_weight it is the two-component mixture (1 - w) F(exp_value) + w F(recent_value) of nba_player_props; its points components use the mixture's own SD law (recent_mixture in ~sportsdataverse.nba.nba_prediction_constants.LEAGUE_CONSTANTS).

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription

statstrOne of "pts", "reb", "ast", "fg3m", "stl", "blk", "tov", "fgm", "ftm".
league_idstr'00'"00" NBA / "10" WNBA / "20" G-League; selects that league's fitted SD law and dispersion.
recent_valuefloat | NoneNoneThe recent-window mean (stat_<stat>_recent_exp).
recent_weightfloat | NoneNoneThe mixture weight (recent_weight); None or 0 gives the single distribution.

Returns

(family, params) where family is "normal", "nbinom" or "poisson"; or "mixture" with params {"weights": [1 - w, w], "components": [(family, params), (family, params)]}.

ratings_as_of(model: 'AnyModel', possessions: 'pl.DataFrame', asof: 'datetime.date') -> 'RatingsFit'

Fit model on every possession dated on or before asof and return ratings.

This is the through-date primitive: possessions with game_date > asof are excluded from the fit entirely (never merely down-weighted), which is what makes the panel built from repeated calls to this function leakage-free by construction — see tests/nba/test_nba_ratings_panel.py::test_ratings_as_of_is_leakage_free_append_invariant. Parameters

asofdateThe through-date checkpoint (inclusive: game_date <= asof). A pregame consumer of a game on day D passes D - 1 day; the pregame surfaces (nba_player_props, nba_team_ratings) take a strict as_of_date instead.

Returns

RatingsFit with per-player offense/defense ratings (per-100-possession scale, same sign convention as nba_rapm: positive d_ratings means good defense). Empty dicts when no possessions fall on or before asof or when possessions is empty.

base_fg_pct is the make chance: model="location" (NBA default) is add_shot_make_prob on loc_x / loc_y / action_type / shot_type for twos, the zone value for threes and unlocated shots; model="zone" (WNBA / G League default, no booster) is the LeagueAverages zone-triple FG% (unmatched: the shot_zone_range mean). Walk-forward (tools/models/nba_shot_value/shot_make_nba_holdout.json) its log loss is 3% to 4% under the zone table's every season since 2018-19, nearly all at the rim; its level misses by over a point on 2018-19, 2020-21 and 2022-23 (owner-accepted).
