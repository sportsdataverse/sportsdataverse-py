nbl_rosetta_seasons​

Every season of an organisation: regular, preseason, in-season cups and test seasons, with dates.

Endpoint URL: GET https://prod.rosetta.nbl.com.au/get/{org}/seasons

Valid URL: https://prod.rosetta.nbl.com.au/get/nbl/seasons

API ParameterPythonPatternRequiredNullableDescription
orgorgYOrganisation: nbl (men's NBL), wnbl (women's) or nbl1 (the second tier, men's and women's conferences).
limitlimitYRows to return. Omitted = a page large enough for the whole list (a short page cut by Rosetta's path cache is refused).

Returns​

return_parsed=True (default) — a tidy polars.DataFrame with the columns below; pass return_as_pandas=True for a pandas.DataFrame.

col_nametypedescription
orgStringOrganisation the row belongs to: nbl, wnbl or nbl1. From the row's competition, else from the request.
season_idStringDataCore season id (UUID v1); accepted as {season_id}.
rosetta_season_idStringRosetta season id (UUID v4; v5 on NBL1); the {season_id} route key.
nameStringSeason name as Rosetta spells it (NBL26, WNBL 2024/25, 2025 South Men); names drift, key on season_year.
season_yearInt64Rosetta's year of the season: the START year for the NBL and WNBL from 1998-99 (2025 = 2025-26), the calendar year for NBL1 and the 1979-1997 winter seasons. See season_end_year for the sdv key.
season_typeStringSeason type: regular (includes finals), preseason, in_season (cups, blitz, exhibition), test.
start_dateStringFirst day of the season, ISO date-time without offset.
end_dateStringLast day of the season, ISO date-time without offset; null on older seasons.
competition_league_nameStringName of the league the season's competition belongs to (NBL, WNBL or NBL1).
competition_divisionStringNBL1 divisions (conference x gender); empty for NBL and WNBL.
season_end_yearInt64sdv season key: the END year of the season (2026 = 2025-26), derived from season_year (a START year for the NBL and WNBL from 1998-99, a calendar year for NBL1 and the 1979-1997 winter seasons; year 1998 is split on the start date). Where the body carries no year it is the season asked for.

return_parsed=False — the raw JSON Dict payload, unparsed.

Raises​

ExceptionDescription
NoDataErrorThe host answered 404 -- the requested resource does not exist. Rosetta answered 404 or no rows (an unknown match, team, player or season id, an org or season type with nothing, or no season in progress).
AssetFetchErrorThe fetch failed (a non-2xx answer or a connection failure after retries, e.g. 401/403/429/5xx, or an empty or unreadable 200 body) -- the answer is unknown, not empty. The 403 gate body, a stub or error envelope, a page cut by Rosetta's path cache, or a body for another org, season or entity than the one asked for.
ValueErrorThe host answered 400 / 422 -- the request is wrong; retrying cannot help.

Example​

nbl_rosetta_seasons(org='nbl')
