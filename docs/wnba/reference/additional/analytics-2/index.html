wnba_official_seasons​

wnba_official_seasons(seasons: 'int | list[int]', *, season_type: 'int | None' = 2, return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

WNBA official season profiles: games, fouls and FTA per game, pace and home splits.

One row per official and season over the completed games of season_type the official worked. fouls_per_game and fta_per_game are both teams' totals from ESPN's team box; pace is possessions (FGA - ORB + TOV + 0.44 FTA, averaged over the two teams) per 40 minutes, over the games' minutes including overtime; home_win_pct and home_minus_away_fouls (home fouls less away fouls, per game) leave out neutral-site games. Every rate has <m>_n, the games it is computed over. The release keys officials by ESPN's official_id.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
seasonsint | list[int]A season or list of seasons, named by the year it ends (the WNBA season is its calendar year).
season_typeint | None2ESPN's season type: 2 regular season (default), 3 postseason; None keeps every type.
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas frame instead of polars.

Returns

wnba_player_game_expected_box(seasons: 'int | list[int]', *, return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

WNBA points, rebounds and assists per player-game against his season, per minute and usage-adjusted.

For every played player-game: <s>_base is his season per-minute rate times the game's minutes; <s>_exp scales that by the game's usage and minutes against his season's (a Poisson model fitted on earlier seasons, scored on later held-out ones). Both use the season without this game (over the regular season (a playoff or play-in game is measured against it)), so a game is never in its own baseline. <s>_exp is null before the model's first held-out season and where a usage is unknown.

wnba_player_season_box_rates​

wnba_player_season_box_rates(seasons: 'int | list[int]', *, return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

WNBA per-36 points, rebounds and assists per player-season, ranked, with the season usage.

Over the regular season (a playoff or play-in game is measured against it), from ESPN's player box. pts_per36 / reb_per36 / ast_per36 are 36 x the total / minutes, each with _n (the minutes) and _rank / _pct among the season's players whose minutes reach the rate's gate (the minutes at which it is half signal on odd / even games). usg_pct is the season usage (FGA + 0.44 FTA + TOV over the team's while on the floor), not ranked.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
seasonsint | list[int]A season or list of seasons, named by the year it ends (2025 is the 2025 season).
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas frame instead of polars.

Returns

One row per (season, athlete_id) (the ESPN id): team_id (his last game's team), games, minutes, pts / reb / ast, usg_pct with usg_pct_n, and the three per-36 rates with _n / _rank / _pct. A season without a release returns that schema with zero rows.

col_nametypedescription
seasonInt64Season, named by the year it ends (the WNBA season is its calendar year).
athlete_idInt64ESPN athlete id of the player, the join key to the player box score.
team_idInt64ESPN team id of the player's last game of the season (a traded player has one row).
gamesInt64Games played (the pros' regular season, every college game).
minutesFloat64Minutes played in those games.
ptsInt64Points in those games.
rebInt64Rebounds in those games.
astInt64Assists in those games.
usg_pctFloat64Season usage: 100 x sum(uses) / sum(minutes x team uses / (team minutes / 5)), uses = FGA + 0.44 FTA + TOV; not ranked.

ast_per36_nInt64Minutes behind ast_per36.

pts_per36_pctFloat64Percentile of pts_per36 among the same qualifiers, 100 (n + 1 - rank) / (n + 1).
reb_per36_rankFloat64Rank of reb_per36 in the league-season (1 best, average ties) among players whose minutes reach its gate (split-half r = 0.5); null under the gate or with fewer than 10 qualifiers.
