mbb_tournament_ineligible​

mbb_tournament_ineligible(seasons: 'list[int] | None' = None, *, league: 'str | None' = None, return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

Division I basketball programs that could not play in the NCAA tournament, per season.

A bundled, hand-curated list (2010-2027): programs reclassifying to Division I (four seasons from Division II until 2025, three since the NCAA's January 2025 rule; St. Thomas came from Division III), and APR, NCAA-infractions and self-imposed postseason bans. Every row cites its source. The college season products (bracketology, season sim, strength of schedule) use it by default to set eligible.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
seasonslist[int] | NoneNoneSeasons to keep, as the end year (2024 is 2023-24). None keeps all.
leaguestr | NoneNone"mens", "womens", or None for both.
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas DataFrame instead of polars.

Returns

One row per ineligible (season, league, team_id): season, league, team_id (ESPN team id, Utf8), team, reason (reclassifying, apr_ban, infractions_ban, self_imposed_ban or other), detail, source_url and confidence (high / medium).

col_nametypedescription
seasonInt64Season, as the end year (2024 is 2023-24).
leagueStringmens or womens.
team_idStringESPN team id (Utf8).
teamStringSchool name of the barred program, as ESPN spells it.
reasonStringWhy the program was barred: reclassifying, apr_ban, infractions_ban, self_imposed_ban or other.
detailStringShort note on the row, such as the reclassification year or the case behind the ban.
source_urlStringThe source that documents the row.
confidenceStringHow well the source documents the row: high (the NCAA, the school or the conference says so) or medium (a secondary source).

Raises

ExceptionDescription
ValueErrorIf league is not "mens", "womens" or None.

Example

from sportsdataverse.mbb import mbb_tournament_ineligible
barred = mbb_tournament_ineligible([2023], league="mens")

# Pipeline next step (one line)

barred.filter(pl.col("reason") == "reclassifying").get_column("team").to_list()

mbb_tournament_runs(seasons: 'int | list[int]', *, league: 'str' = 'mens', return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

Every tournament game, once per side, keyed to that team's run, with the run's record after it.

A run is one team's games in one tournament. The games are the postseason (ESPN season_type 3: the NCAA tournament from the First Four, the NIT / WNIT, the CBI, the Crown, the WBIT ...) and the conference tournaments (a tournament_id with a conference group, from mid-February). run_id is "{season}-{tournament_key}-{team_id}"; tournament_key is ESPN's tournament_id, or the headline's event name where the id is missing (older seasons). A conference tournament ESPN did not tag is not a run.

ncaa_coach_stints​

ncaa_coach_stints(coaches: 'pl.DataFrame', records: 'pl.DataFrame') -> 'pl.DataFrame'

The card coach of each team-season as a whole-season stint, unless the card says the season changed hands.

A card whose season record (coach_season_records) is short of the team's by more than a game and more than a loss marks a mid-season change, and its figures are not the card coach's own games: on Sports-Reference they are the other coach's (Ole Miss 2017-18's card names Andy Kennedy with 1-4, Tony Madlock's five games; Kennedy went 11-16). Such a team-season gets no stint, so it stays uncredited (and is counted in a log line). Otherwise -- the record matches, is a loss off, drops vacated wins (never losses), or cannot be computed -- the card coach has the whole season.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
coachesDataFramencaa_team_coaches rows, with the season before each wanted one for the record difference.
recordsDataFrameThe teams' season records: season, team_id, games, losses (as sportsdataverse._common.team_luck.season_records gives them).

Returns

Stints in ~sportsdataverse._common.coach_seasons.STINTS_SCHEMA with no counts and position="any", one per team-season with a card, an ESPN team id (cards with none are counted in a log line) and no mid-season change.

col_nametypedescription
seasonInt64Season, named by the year it ends (2025 is 2024-25).
team_idInt64ESPN team id of the school (from the card's NCAA team-season crosswalk).
coach_idInt64NCAA person id of the card's head coach (stats.ncaa.org /people/{id}).
coachStringHead coach's name on the card.
gamesInt64Always null here; the card coach has the whole season (a season the card shows changing hands has no stint).
winsInt64Always null here, as games.

ncaa_team_game_counts​

ncaa_team_game_counts(pbp: 'pl.DataFrame') -> 'pl.DataFrame'

Each team's shots, free throws, rebounds and turnovers per game, whole and with garbage time split out.

Sums the stats.ncaa.org events by the team that made them (event_team_espn_team_id): a field goal is a shot_value 2 or 3 event, a free throw a shot_value 1 event, made when event_result is made; oreb / dreb / tov count the Offensive Rebound / Defensive Rebound / Turnover events (team rebounds and team turnovers included, Deadball Rebound not). The garbage split is each event's own is_garbage_time (bigballR's flag, the one the possessions release carries); an event with no flag is in all only.

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
pbpDataFrameload_ncaa_mbb_pbp / load_ncaa_wbb_pbp rows.

Returns

One row per (season, game_id, team_id, family, split): family all (split all) and family garbage (garbage_time, not_garbage_time, a split with no event of the team has no row), with game_id the stats.ncaa.org contest id (Utf8), team_id the ESPN team id (Int64; an event of a team without one, a non-D1 side, is dropped) and the COUNT_COLUMNS (Int64). Within a game the two garbage rows add up to the all row when every event is flagged. An empty input returns that schema with zero rows.

col_nametypedescription
seasonInt64Season year (the year the season ends).
game_idStringThe stats.ncaa.org contest id.
team_idInt64ESPN team id of the team that made the events; events of a side with no ESPN id (non-D1) are dropped.
familyStringall (the whole game) or garbage.
splitStringall, or garbage_time / not_garbage_time by each event's is_garbage_time.
fgaInt64Field-goal attempts in the split: the team's shot_value 2 and 3 events in the ncaa pbp.
fgmInt64Made field goals in the split (event_result made).
fg3aInt64Three-point attempts in the split (shot_value 3).
fg3mInt64Made threes in the split.
ftaInt64Free-throw attempts in the split (shot_value 1).
ftmInt64Made free throws in the split.
orebInt64Offensive rebounds in the split (Offensive Rebound events, team rebounds included).
drebInt64Defensive rebounds in the split (Defensive Rebound events, team rebounds included).
tovInt64Turnovers in the split (Turnover events, team turnovers included).
