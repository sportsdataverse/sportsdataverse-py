mbb_player_war​

mbb_player_war(seasons: 'int | list[int]', *, league: 'str' = 'mens', return_as_pandas: 'bool' = False) -> 'pl.DataFrame | pd.DataFrame'

WAR and VORP per player-season, from our box BPM.

Box BPM is anchored to the team and centred on Division I, so 0 is an average D1 player. vorp is points per 100 team possessions above a replacement player over one team's season of possessions (Basketball-Reference's VORP scale); war is wins above replacement against an average D1 schedule. A replacement-level player has 0, and a season's war adds up to the D1 teams' wins minus those of a team of replacement players. The replacement level and points per win are fitted per league (~sportsdataverse._common.player_war.REPLACEMENT_LEVEL, ~sportsdataverse._common.player_war.PTS_PER_WIN).

Parameters

ParameterTypeDefaultDescription
seasonsint | list[int]A season or list of seasons, named by the year it ends (2026 is 2025-26).
leaguestr'mens'"mens" or "womens" (sportsdataverse.wbb.wbb_player_war.wbb_player_war).
return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas frame instead of polars.

Returns

One row per (season, player_id) (ESPN ids, String) of a D1 team -- one with 10 or more games in ~sportsdataverse.mbb.mbb_team_ratings.mbb_team_ratings, box BPM's own centring set, so a season is empty until its teams reach 10 games: player_name, team_id (the team he played most possessions for), teams, min, poss (minutes / 40 * the team's adjusted tempo), rating (anchored box BPM, per 100), vorp and war with war_lo / war_hi (a nominal 90% interval from a bootstrap over each team-season's games: the sampling uncertainty of the team side and the playing time, not of box BPM itself), with _rank (1 = most), _pct and _n (possessions) for vorp and war among every player of the season. Sorted by season and war. Empty input returns that schema with zero rows.

col_nametypedescription
seasonInt64Season, named by the year it ends (2025 is 2024-25).
player_idStringThe player's ESPN athlete id, as text (the box-score id the college player-value models key on).
player_nameStringPlayer name, from the box score.
team_idStringESPN team id of the Division I team he played the most possessions for.
teamsInt64How many Division I teams he played for.
minFloat64Minutes in the season's box scores.
possFloat64Possessions: minutes / 40 * the team's adjusted tempo (mbb_team_ratings adj_tempo, possessions per 40 minutes). College has no published on-court possessions for every game. The minutes basis of vorp and war.
ratingFloat64Points per 100 possessions above an average Division I player: box BPM (mbb_box_bpm, model 1 of the player-value spine), re-anchored to the team (team mean = adjusted efficiency margin minus the D1 mean, / 5) and centred so the D1 possession-weighted mean is 0.
vorpFloat64Value over replacement player: (rating - R) * poss / the season's possessions per D1 team, R the league's replacement level (REPLACEMENT_LEVEL; MBB -1.66, WBB -1.99 per 100). Basketball-Reference's VORP scale.
warFloat64Wins above replacement against an average D1 schedule: (rating - R) * poss / 100 / points per win (PTS_PER_WIN; MBB 38.91, WBB 47.10). 0 at replacement level; a replacement team wins about .35.
war_loFloat64Low end of the nominal 90% interval for war (5th percentile of 1000 bootstrap replicates, each team-season seeded on its own): each team-season's games resampled with replacement (a game's player possessions, team possessions and margin move together), then his possessions, the team net rating, the anchoring and the centring recomputed. The source ratings and the replacement level are held fixed, so this is the sampling uncertainty of the team side and the playing time (in college the replicate's adjusted net rating is the adjusted rating plus the change in the raw one); box BPM's own sampling error is in no column.
war_hiFloat64High end of the nominal 90% interval for war (95th percentile of the same 1000 game-resampled replicates as war_lo).
vorp_rankFloat64Rank of vorp within the season among every player (1 = most; ties average); null under 10 players.
war_rankFloat64Rank of war within the season among every player (1 = most; ties average); null under 10 players.
vorp_pctFloat64Percentile of vorp within the season: 100 * (n + 1 - rank) / (n + 1), n the players ranked; null when the rank is null.
war_pctFloat64Percentile of war within the season: 100 * (n + 1 - rank) / (n + 1), n the players ranked; null when the rank is null.
vorp_nInt64Sample size of vorp: on-court possessions, rounded.
war_nInt64Sample size of war: on-court possessions, rounded.

Raises

ExceptionDescription
ValueErrorIf league is not "mens" or "womens" (checked before any data is loaded).

Example

from sportsdataverse.mbb.mbb_player_war import mbb_player_war
war = mbb_player_war(2025)
war.select("player_name", "poss", "rating", "vorp", "war").head(10)

The daily strength_of_schedule table: records, SoS, quad résumé, WAB, RPI and a NET estimate.

Loads the released ESPN schedule and team box per season and builds ~sportsdataverse.mbb.mbb_college_season.season_resume from the games strictly before as_of_date: opponent-adjusted ratings, SoS (mean opponent AdjEM), WAB, the NCAA Quad 1-4 record (opponents ranked by the NET estimate), the NCAA RPI (25% WP / 50% OWP / 25% OOWP, location-weighted WP) and the NET-style estimate (0.8 * z(AdjEM) + 0.2 * z(WAB)). Division I teams only; RPI, SoS, WAB and the quads count Division I games only.

return_as_pandasboolFalseReturn a pandas DataFrame instead of polars.

Returns
